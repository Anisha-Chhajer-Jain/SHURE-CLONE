ULX-D

Digital Wireless Systems SKU: ULX-D


Find a Retainer


OVERVIEW

Large conference and convention installations come with unique wireless challenges. ULX-D® Digital Wireless is the trusted and reliable partner for secure, scalable and durable performance, campus-wide. With expanded wide-tuning frequency bands and up to a 40% increase in rechargeable battery run-time, ULX-D Wireless provides intelligent solutions for installations and event requirements of all sizes.
For higher channel counts in high-tier applications, the ANX4 Scalable Wireless Receiver receives up to 24 channels of Shure ULX-D wireless audio in a single rack space.Choose ANX4 to maximise use of your budget, rack space, and rack weight.

Shure Product Benefits

ULX-D continues to push wireless audio further. Expanded tuning ranges that provide more open channels for use region-wide. Higher density, running up to 47 systems simultaneously within 6 MHz. These reasons and more are why ULX-D continues to be the standard bearer in wireless technology for installations and performance events, around the world.

Multiple spectrum options, including VHF, UHF, 900 ISM, 1.2 GHz (Japan only), and 1.5 GHz and 1.8 GHz (Europe only) Wide-tuning handheld and bodypack transmitters Essential boundary and gooseneck transmitter options Choice of receiver type (single or wide-tuning dual and quad) ANX4 Scalable Wireless Receiver allows up to 24 ULX-D channels to be received in one rack space Monitoring and control functionality.

The 24-bit digital audio and the wide frequency response capture the sound as clearly and accurately as it was produced. The network is always yours to command with proven Shure tools like Wireless Workbench®, SystemOn, Axient Spectrum Manager, Confidentiality is ensured via AES-256 encryption that generates a randomised key for each use. ULX-D transmitters use either AA batteries or our intelligent rechargeable solutions for up to 9 hours of use.So power is always on your side.
